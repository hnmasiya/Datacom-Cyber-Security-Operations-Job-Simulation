# Task 1 — Cyberattack Investigation

## Scenario
The simulation involved **Orion Health Services**, a 250-person ANZ healthcare technology provider.

A ransomware incident began with a phishing email containing a malicious Excel attachment.

## Investigation Focus
The work covered:
- Incident timeline and attack-chain reconstruction
- Indicators of compromise
- Containment and credential protection
- Evidence preservation and collection
- Recovery considerations
- Potential data-exposure considerations
- Root-cause analysis
- MITRE ATT&CK mapping
- Security improvement recommendations
- Executive priorities

## Key Indicators
The scenario included an overseas login, Mimikatz activity, and the `.orionlock` file extension.

## Security Themes
The exercise demonstrated the importance of security awareness, identity and credential protection, endpoint monitoring, resilient backups, evidence preservation, structured incident response, and mapping activity to recognized attack techniques.

> This document summarizes work completed in a Forage simulation and does not represent a real incident at Orion Health Services.

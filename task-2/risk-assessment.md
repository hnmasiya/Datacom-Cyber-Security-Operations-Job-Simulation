# Task 2 — Cybersecurity Risk Assessment

## Client
**RetailNova Pty Ltd**, a simulated retail company headquartered in Melbourne, Australia.

The scenario described approximately 1,200 employees, 85 stores, $450 million annual revenue, e-commerce/mobile platforms, AWS, cloud POS, Salesforce, SAP, payment integrations, customer/loyalty data, BYOD, VPN, Teams, Slack, and third-party providers.

## Risk Areas
1. Phishing and credential compromise
2. Ransomware and enterprise disruption
3. Third-party/vendor exposure
4. E-commerce/AWS compromise
5. BYOD and remote-access compromise

## Assessment Approach
A **5×5 likelihood/consequence risk matrix** was used to assess the major cybersecurity risks and identify mitigation priorities.

## Mitigation Themes
- Strong identity and access management
- MFA and least privilege
- Security awareness and phishing training
- Endpoint monitoring and response
- Network/system segmentation
- Resilient backups and recovery testing
- Third-party risk management
- Cloud security monitoring
- Incident response planning and testing

> This document summarizes the simulated RetailNova assessment completed during the Datacom Forage job simulation. It is not an assessment of a real RetailNova environment.
